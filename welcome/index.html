Why this exists

A big family is hard to keep straight

What you can do

Built for how our family talks

Your title on every card

Tap your own name and each relative shows what you call them: Bác, Chú, O, Cậu, Dì, Anh, Chị, Em, Cháu. Turn on Miền Trung and Ba's younger sister reads O instead of Cô.

Every generation on one wheel

The oldest couple sits in the centre and each ring out is the next generation. Turn it, zoom it, or snap to two rings.

Side, top and simple views

Open any family as a chart: their parents, the couple, and every generation below them.

Faces, not only names

Send a photo from your phone and it replaces the drawing on that card, marked Pending until Hạnh confirms it.

Fix it when you spot it

Add a new baby, a spouse, or a missing name with Edit Tree. Each change shows as Pending until Hạnh reviews it.

Trees that meet

The same person in two trees is one person. The Multiverse stacks up to five trees in 3D and shows who links them.

How it works

Three taps to find your place

  1. 1

    Open the tree

    No account and no sign in. The wheel opens on Hạnh's Paternal Grandpa & Siblings.

  2. 2

    Find yourself

    Search your name in Options, accents optional, or tap your card. Titles appear on everyone around you.

  3. 3

    Add what is missing

    Use Edit Tree or Add a picture. You type the family code once per phone, and Hạnh checks every change.

Questions

Before you open it

Can't find your answer? Ask Hạnh.

Do I need an account?
No. Anyone with the link can look. Only adding or fixing something asks for the family code, and only once per phone.
How do I find myself?
Open Options and type your name. Accents don't matter, so "hanh" finds Hạnh. Results say whose child or spouse each person is.
Why does the title say Bác and not Chú?
Titles follow vai: which branch is older where the lines split, not who is older in years. Relatives by marriage go by age.
What does Miền Trung change?
With it on, Ba's younger sister is O, as Central families say. Turn it off for Cô.
What do the red numbers and the wings mean?
A red number is birth order, oldest first. Marigold wings mean that person has passed.
Why does a card say ~INC~?
Nobody has filled in that name yet. If you know it, add it with Edit Tree.
How do I add a photo?
Use Add a picture, pick the person, fit the face in the oval and send. It shows on the card right away, marked Pending.
Who can see the tree?
Anyone with the link can see the main trees, names and all. Trees on the editors' page stay private until their editor publishes them.
